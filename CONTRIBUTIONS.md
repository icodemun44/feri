- Tasks completed: all application code, the database, the project setup, reviews and merges, and the project documents.

- What I built:
  - Foundation: Turborepo workspace, Docker and local Supabase, Prisma migrations with row level security, shared packages for rules, validation and the interface components, CI, and commit checks.
  - Accounts: sign up, log in, and roles for buyer, seller and admin, enforced on routes, pages and in the services.
  - Storefront: home page with a banner carousel, categories, product browse with search and filters, product pages, and shop pages.
  - Selling: seller applications reviewed by an admin with call notes, seller listings with photo upload, and an admin check of new listings.
  - Buying: cart, cash on delivery checkout (one order per seller), buyer orders, and seller order handling from confirm to delivered or cancelled.
  - Reviews: buyer ratings after delivery, seller ratings, and admin removal of reviews with an audit log.
  - Sample data: 77 sample products with photos, and demo accounts.
  - Documents: the requirements document, implementation plan, setup guide, database notes, design system, and these contribution notes.
